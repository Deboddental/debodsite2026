## Voice
Honest dentistry / "odontología honesta": clear, warm, precise, premium — never
salesy, fear-mongering, or absolute ("the only", "the most advanced in the world",
"painless", "lasts a lifetime", unattributed "No. 1"). Prefer "one of the most…",
"typically", conditioned durability, and named, verifiable proof.
